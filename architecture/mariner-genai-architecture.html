Routes

Coaching360 chats are handled first, then slash commands. Performance questions try the fast path; anything it can't answer, and every other question, goes to the Supervisor.

Citations

Agents return references; the app checks them against the chunk tables through the SQL warehouse and links each one to its PDF page.

Precomputed data

The fast-path views and the Coaching360 prepared tables are materialized views refreshed daily, so the app and Genie read them without rebuilding joins.
